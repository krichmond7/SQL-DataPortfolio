# SQL-DataPortfolio
## Findings

### Finding 1: Satisfaction Scores and Attrition
Employees who left the company reported slightly lower average satisfaction across all three measures — Environment Satisfaction, Job Satisfaction, and Work-Life Balance — compared to employees who stayed (who averaged ~2.77–2.78 out of 4 on each). The gap between those who left and those who stayed was modest rather than dramatic, suggesting that low satisfaction alone is not a strong standalone predictor of attrition. This pointed toward testing more structural factors, like overtime, as a next step.
